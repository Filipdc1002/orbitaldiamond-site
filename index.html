Orbital Survival Craft™

Survive the world. Master its resources. Conquer the solar system.

From your first wooden tool to an Interplanetary Automated Network, every advancement is something you build. Orbital Survival Craft is an ambitious open-world survival crafting game that begins on a fully spherical planet and grows into a journey across moons and distant worlds.

Gather materials, find food and fresh water, craft weapons and armor, and build a shelter against hostile creatures and dangerous environments. Explore the horizon in every direction, fight through enemy strongholds, and turn a fragile camp into a thriving home.

The vision spans eight tiers of crafting and technology: increasingly powerful tools and equipment, energy generation and distribution, sophisticated machinery, and automated resource production. Construct your first rocket, leave your home planet behind, and establish outposts where unfamiliar resources open the door to new possibilities.

Eventually, connect operations across worlds with interplanetary logistics, develop immense energy infrastructure, and work toward the ability to travel between planets using advanced teleportation technology. Yet no matter how powerful your network becomes, survival never stops — food, water, combat and exploration remain essential.

Survive. Craft. Explore. Automate. Expand.

Truly spherical worlds

Explore entire round planets with varied landscapes, wildlife, hostile creatures and hidden opportunities.

Survival at every stage

Manage hunger and thirst, craft equipment, build shelter and face danger — even when your technology reaches the stars.

Eight tiers of progression

Advance from primitive tools to advanced alloys, powered machines, rockets and high-energy technologies. Planned progression is driven by crafting, not research trees.

Build an interplanetary network

Planned: reach moons, colonize new worlds, automate production and link distant outposts through logistics and advanced travel.
