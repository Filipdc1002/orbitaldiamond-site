Project Veridian (working title)

A survival RPG on a fully round planet. Crash-land in an escape pod, gather and craft your way from stone to diamond, build a home that holds, and get ready for the dragon waiting at the south pole.

A whole round world

Walk, ride or fly all the way around a planet with forests, snow, seas, rivers and waterfalls.

Stone to diamond

Climb four crafting tiers, from a first hatchet to diamond armour, a rifle and a jetpack.

Build anywhere

Foundations, walls, vaults and workbenches. Wood, stone and metal each stand up to different threats.

Ride, fly, fight

Tame and saddle an elephant, take to the sky, fish the lakes and raid goblin fortresses.
